# ADR-0053: Construct a joint first contour and interior-hatch candidate

Status: locally verified; independent review pending.

## Invariant

Construct one complete prospective first candidate from the original actual
body/target: four closed contour edges, followed by qualified original interior
hatch owners. Replace boundary owners rather than appending duplicate outer
hatches. Trim each interior finite butt to the contour centre extents, re-prove
its whole footprint/amount, and measure joint S/U/R/C/M/spill in one fresh ledger.
Preserve original source, later prospective paths and the boundary band.

## Decision

Reuse one private qualified constructor for contour geometry and first-bead
proofs. Own four starting vertices/directions as before. All first hatch owners
must have the declared contour width; original generation policy remains
provenance after width replanning. Select interior centres strictly between the
contour sides. Record every replaced boundary index; retain each selected
original index. Exact affine endpoint lifting and outward storage errors keep
finite interior butts within their original parent extents. Reject a request
with no interior hatch, incompatible width/extent or any failed later path.

The four contour edges and all interior paths share packet/roof/error/work and
root/nested deadlines from the start. Divide the original packet volume budget
among all selected paths before reconstruction. Retain active-prefix/future
semantics, immutable inputs and callbacks through publication. No independently
measured component is summed into a fabricated joint fill. Reuse the complete
candidate measurement: actual path order, nonzero unqualified travel connectors,
one fresh cap ledger, complete nominal box and final joint union/fill. Section
targets remain distinct from whole-cell target and commanded amounts.

## Qualified multiple-row union

Extend the existing full-box, rounded, axis-aligned loop kernel to multiple
congruent primary rows. Canonicalize forward/reverse packets and require identical
finite extents, actual amounts, affine top/bottom/gap and continuous packet joins
for every primary group. At every packet require each adjacent centre separation
within the minimum actual flat core. The outer-pair separation alone may exceed
that core; only the certified chain of all intervening cores fills the central
span. Two constant transverse ends must meet the actual outer endpoints and
remain disjoint. For primary centre span B,

    U_primary = sum(V_one + B*L*(h0+h1)/2).
    U = U_primary + V_end0 + V_end1 - I0 - I1.

Retain the existing bounded stadium/affine-strip XZ intersection integrals for
I0/I1, exact accumulated amounts and original shared precision/cell/work/depth/
deadline limits. Calculate R = S-U for total multiplicity, including triple
occupancy; never sum pair intersections. Select the primary axis from the actual
multiple-row/end layout, allowing the infill direction to be the shorter axis.
Unequal interior profiles, unmerged adjacent cores, current partial events,
clipping or real packet gaps retain the general solver; UNKNOWN refuses.

## Contract and boundaries

Add protected first-cap contract 1. First-bead 5, contour 1 and union/fill meaning
remain unchanged: contour edges have no infill owner, interior intervals retain
their original owner, and all material quantities still measure their declared
nominal sets. Rebuild all consumers; no persisted cache/IR/3MF/profile format or
public default changes. Additional group validation changes work accounting;
historical reports retain their own revision counts.

This prospective construction does not replace actually laid partial material,
qualify allowable corner/infill overlap or joining contact, select legal head
motion/order/flow, fill remaining 3D voids, reconstruct later actual support or
permit export. Infill is kept in original forward source order; no claim of a
complete direction scheduler or downstream order protection is made. Full B01-B15
remains active and the public export gate stays blocked.

## Evidence

Both axes and flat/sloped first surfaces pass. Independent 113-bit stadium
amounts and the merged-core closed-loop identity enclose S/U/R/C; flat candidates
have positive measured C gain/M reduction against a contour on the same target.
An independent multiple-row oracle proves total multiplicity and rejects an
unequal/misplaced/partial interior. A local ridge outside the contour footprints
refuses the later infill, with future material absent. Native evidence uses the
same unchanged sliced wedge and actual body; a wider ROI is independently proven
inside an actual nominal flat core, not substituted by a slab or a changed model.
The wider native target is separate from prior narrow-ROI metrics.

Final commands, failures, XML and source/binary/raw hashes are recorded in
B07-first-cap.md. Independent review, Windows and physical qualification remain
NOT_RUN; local numerical checks do not qualify the real U1.
