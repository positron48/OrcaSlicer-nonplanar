# B09 lifted Travel: separate author critical review

This records an author review of the actual implementation, source and retained
failures. The independent critical review required by Gate A/B remains pending.

The source snapshot and limits are captured before callbacks. The source was
created by protected preparation factories; the raw cloned journal does not
reuse caller-provided derived geometry. Both existing preparations rerun over
every original and inserted record, enforcing pressure and pose continuity,
bounded source size, scene applicability and the complete potential Upper height.
The original policy is retained, avoiding repeated scene-error inflation.

The only edited payload is the selected Travel's poses and the IDs of its added
legs. All deposited volume, width/gap/flux metadata and pressure rows retain their
values and order. IDs are chosen above every original ID, with overflow checked;
chronology is the explicit vector/index order, not numerical ID order. Tests
compare unchanged canonical records, normalizing only the intentional shifted
index. Retract/unretract around the route retain their amounts and states. No
pressure restoration is misclassified as new material.

Every new event is checked at its exact new index with every captured component
and obstacle and the complete original deposited prefix. Future rows never become
obstacles merely because the journal was revalidated. Whole swept material cells
and continuous static queries cover each full leg. Omitting duplicate points
does not make an instant Travel vacuously safe: it retains one instant obligation.
All successful leg certificates and a final callback check are necessary for the
private route snapshot. A failed or UNKNOWN leg can retain diagnostic source data,
but never a complete route snapshot.

Total work includes both preparations and failed checks. Cells and static pairs
accumulate across legs; the pair-budget test supplies 41 for 42 required pairs,
and the work-budget test withholds the final leg's work. Both refuse. Children
receive remaining budgets and callbacks enforce the original absolute deadline
and revisions. Stale source/scene, cancellation, late publication, invalid rounding,
low or out-of-domain height, missing/wrong event, record limits and ID overflow
refuse. No safety policy or original negative is weakened.

Independent 113-bit inequalities enclose the entire old affine bead, annulus,
six head boxes and static obstacles throughout the positive three-leg route.
The old direct Travel intersects the wall; the lifted route passes with its full
seven-component head. Separate obstacles intersect the interior of exit/descent
although both endpoints are clear; long-double swept-box arithmetic checks the
reported witnesses. These are analytical whole-domain checks, not reusing the
planner as an oracle. Existing material-motion negatives remain.

The first native height proposal of 3 mm was below the actual 4.61022 mm start.
A new proposal at 6.12872 mm is above the full 6.02872 mm Upper ceiling and below
the original nozzle-domain maximum. Its complete exit still returns UNKNOWN,
MATERIAL_MOTION_UNCERTAIN_BOUNDARY, without a collision witness. The test now
requires that actual refusal and all original canonical rows; this is not a
proven physical collision or a positive native exit. The analytical positive and
interior-collision negatives remain. All intermediate failed commands are kept.

Only replacement legs are qualified. Other original motions, full cap/contact/
tool access and order, resource/software provenance, final candidate replay and
physical evidence remain separate obligations. No persisted proof/cache or
export path was added. macOS verification, Linux observations and NOT_RUN Windows
are recorded separately in the report and manifest; full B completion is not claimed.
