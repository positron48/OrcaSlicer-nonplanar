# ADR-0049: Construct and measure the complete first-hatch candidate

Status: locally verified; Linux and independent review pending.

## Invariant

Every first-pass hatch must be constructed against the same protected actual
body prefix, under one shared amount-error/work/deadline budget. Measure the
complete prospective nominal union together. Summing individual extrusion or
per-line coverage cannot prove useful fill when new lines overlap.

## Decision

Add the internal `first_hatch_layer_contract_version=1` factory and immutable
snapshot. Capture source, box and limits before callbacks. Validate original
nested limits before reducing them. Construct every source line with the existing
continuous finite-width roof/section solver; refusal of any line rejects the
whole candidate. Packet volume error, packet/roof counts and bead work are shared
across all lines. Sum actual section-target and commanded-amount bounds with
exact arithmetic and check the global amount error again.

Create a fresh prospective ledger with declared deposition metadata from an
actually present body record. Retain the declared source context; the owned
hatch source and fill target separately retain the exact actual body prefix.
Do not relabel the old partial cap prefix as this new complete candidate. The
new ledger's connectors and proposed sequence are unqualified travel/order.
Require every complete nominal packet and the target to lie inside the exact
requested XY/Z domain; no clipping may conceal omitted material. Numerical
section errors remain charged in the captured material model.

For two congruent parallel stadium sections separated by d no greater than
their flat-core width, the union is a stadium of width w+d and area A+h*d.
The kernel uses that exact identity only for two equal, aligned axis packet
groups, complete nominal XYZ containment, equal actual commanded amounts,
tip/gap endpoints and interior-disjoint longitudinal intervals in each group.
Prove the core-width condition with outward actual area/gap bounds. Integrate
affine h with exact arithmetic; retain longitudinal gaps. Sort/comparison and
pair checks consume the original work/deadline limits. Partial, clipped, unequal
or more distant sections keep the existing general integrator. Failed probe/
butt-partition trials are discarded; they supply no accepted certificate.

Integrate the new/new union before reconciling it with the protected whole-cell
first-pass target. Preserve individual amount S, occupied U, multiplicity excess
R=S-U, covered C, missing M and below/above spill. The section-target sum is a
separate quantity from the whole-cell target. Preserve positive M/R instead of
fabricating nominal quotas or claiming complete filling. Source/hash walks,
record capture and union/fill consume the shared root work/cell/time budget;
nested stop and revision callbacks remain active throughout.

## Consumers and limits

This is an additive internal API called by native/analytical tests. Existing
contract versions, cache/serialization, profiles, 3MF, IR and public export do
not change; no persisted migration is required. There is no production planner
caller or export authorization yet. Do not append this fresh candidate to an
already partial cap ledger.

This resolves complete first-list construction and measurement only. Remaining
3D space/global replanning, sequential qualified floor/contact, seam/edges,
curved/later actual support, complete head CCD and motion/order/flow, job/plate/
software provenance and final-byte replay/export remain required for full B.
The unchanged Linux native-fill deadline must be verified separately. No
numerical, work, time or geometry limits are relaxed.

## Verification

Independent stadium-overlap measures cover both first axes and enclose S/U/R/C
while retaining real M. A current off-axis ridge that only intersects the second
line refuses the complete candidate; future material and the current event past
its true butt remain absent. Mutation, clipped/mismatched domains, shared path/
record/packet/roof/work/cell limits, stale revision, cancellation and rounding
refuse snapshots. An actual native flat-core fixture constructs both first lines
and independently encloses actual S/U/R, bounded section-target amounts, positive
overlap/deficit and coverage gain versus
its separate old partial cap measurement. Commands, results and source/binary
hashes are recorded in `B07-first-hatch-layer.md` and its evidence archive.
