## Result and native hook

The analytical wedge is split by native `cut_mesh` at z=1 before a derived Model
enters `Print::apply`/`Print::process`. Tests observe 124 mm3 original = 80 mm3
body + 44 mm3 cap (float mesh tolerance 1e-4), unchanged source vertices, and
native body layers ending at z=1 instead of rebuilding the original roof.
Actual native top-solid ExtrusionPath segments provide the support cores.

The 4 by 0.4 mm candidate has a nonzero affine height change, nominal volume
approximately 0.32 mm3 and a valid gap over the 1 mm terrace. Its uncertain
volume fits inside the reserved cap budget. Over the independently sliced
0.8 mm terrace, the same absolute heights produce a gap greater than 0.3 mm
and are rejected. These are terrace interiors, not a solved crossing seam.

An independent affine integral checks all corners, XY volume (0.704 mm3),
transverse slope, offset mutations, material uncertainty and uncovered areas.
The translated native fixture is integrated from its actual binary64 input
coordinates; decimal construction rounding is not added to production bounds.
See ADR-0007 and the separate author review in `A05-critical-review.md`.
