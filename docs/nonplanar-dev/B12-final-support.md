# B12 final-byte whole-run support and actual gap bands

Invariant: ADR-0074. The independent verifier reconstructs every actual target
packet's affine carrier from final decimal XYZ and a separate explicit synthetic
transverse slope. It queries only actual material completed before the selected
run. Internal target packets cannot become their own underlying support, and
future/travel/pressure rows cannot supply material. Every packet retains its
own decimal dose, actual fraction, section and conservative complete finite XY
footprint; widths and slopes are not averaged across packet boundaries.

Whole parameter tiles enclose every vertical/normal ray through the original
minimum in empty Nominal boxes. Every normal maximum endpoint box must be
covered by actual Nominal material; every vertical maximum endpoint must have
joined Lower cover. Lower is separate from Nominal and supplies a guaranteed
geometric anchor, not a physical void/contact/delivery claim. The complete
partition, protected original source/run owners and both cover certificates are
retained. A terminal below thin material may conservatively refuse even if a
first intersection exists. The bands are not expanded to make terminals fit.

The query allowance is four times the original numerical coordinate error and
bounds the full allowance cube Euclidean radius by the original .05 mm cap.
It inflates queries, never new material. Normal direction does not rotate the
fixed tool. Negative witnesses require a point
certified inside the actual nominal target footprint, followed by whole-box
membership of its ray or endpoint. Sampled positives cannot accept a tile.
Freshness/numeric/cancellation/deadline/work/cell/depth guards retain no partial
positive proof or negative witness on UNKNOWN.
