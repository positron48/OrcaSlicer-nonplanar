# ADR-0080: own one prospective cap bead and its complete departure

Status: bounded B09 simulation contract implemented; full B01–B15, independent
review and physical qualification remain pending. Scope: ProfileScene and its
two native test consumers. Normative documents and production formats are intact.

The original .38 mm native later bead has a declared Upper intersection with
the annulus at the start of its exit. A higher ceiling cannot repair it. Keep
that journal, candidate bytes and independently checked FAIL witness. Construct
a separate prospective candidate from the complete parent before that bead.
Width selection, legal deposition contact and full target filling remain open.

`plan_simulation_cap_departure` accepts an already computed NextCapBead owner,
its exact complete FirstCapMaterial parent, a synthetic scene, original policy,
an explicit destination/lift height and resource limits. The bead and its normal
spacing proof must refer to that exact parent graph, not an equal-content copy
or a journal which has already laid the original bead. The factory copies all
inputs and callbacks before invoking them. It does not compute/select a width.

Append the selected bead through append_next_cap_material, which retains the
complete body/first-cap journal, ordered material ownership, original cap and
cell targets, support and recalculated packet doses/sections. Append one new
Travel with an unused event ID. Revalidate the full ledger and original whole
head through the existing preparation factories. The existing lifted-travel
factory replaces that Travel with complete lift/transfer/descent obligations,
retaining every earlier canonical row and a complete origin map. Only a complete
continuous proof for all actual new-prefix/head/static pairs constructs the
private immutable SimulationCapDepartureSnapshot. Travel has no contact exception.

Assembly, cloning, both preparations and all route queries share the original
total work, record and absolute time limits; child allowances only decrease.
Cells/static pairs are shared by all route legs. Child material limits also
remain in force. Precomputed bead/support construction work is retained in its
owner and is outside this assembly/route call's allowance. Cancellation,
material/scene revision checks and floating-point environment are checked
through nested calls and before publication. Latch callback exceptions, including
non-standard or empty-message exceptions, so a child catch cannot recover a
successful output. Any late refusal clears the complete snapshot.

The test-only independent 113-bit oracle derives whole Travel-cell physical
bounds and the declared Upper cross-section from equations, without planner
interval/membership/query code. It checks every bead/head pair, containment,
pairwise disjoint leaf interiors and complete time × local-domain measure.
Its restricted analytical coordinate/height/length/volume domain and separate
1e-20 reference exclusion guard are explicit; a 1e-28 measure accumulation
allowance is unrelated to the planner margin. Points never certify these routes.

The native .28 mm prospective variant uses the unchanged complete body/first cap,
whole later target cell, .4 mm opening / 1 mm outer butt at local Z=0, six original
rigid head parts, original required margin, model losses, budgets and destination
height. The smaller actual bead dose does not fill or replace the whole original
cap target. This fixed-width diagnostic variant does not enable variable-width
job planning or establish a printable .28 mm line on the user's nozzle.

Recapture its departure using the existing explicit 2e-6 conversion allowance,
then run the existing motion planner/serializer and independent final-byte rate,
pose, dose and material reconstruction. These component results do not qualify
final-byte head/contact geometry. A .2 mm prospective variant passes departure
clearance but fails final rounded-section reconstruction at the unchanged 2%
delivered-dose uncertainty; retain that negative rather than narrowing the error.

`simulation_cap_departure_version` is 1. Existing runtime contracts, profiles,
3MF, candidate/report formats and mandatory seventeen-check registry do not
change. The new journal has an ordinary changed fingerprint; it cannot reuse an
old clearance/job certificate. No persisted proof cache or export path is added.

Remaining requirements include one complete fixed-width cap policy/fill/seam,
legal print contact/access, full scheduling/height selection, original prolog/
parking/wipe/end motions, qualified firmware transforms/flow/head/material,
independent final-byte geometry and exact native job/attempt graph binding.
The current B13 native capture rejects these extra route rows; a future explicit
protected departure edge must bind them without loosening canonical source
identity. Thirteen mandatory full-job domains stay UNKNOWN/NOT_RUN and guarded
export stays BLOCK. Synthetic owners never become operator-confirmed.
