# ADR-0058: Retain central cap packets while qualifying two new end strips

Status: locally verified; independent review pending.

## Invariant

Replace one complete prospective first cap on its original actual body and
protected target. Retain all four contour snapshots and every original central
infill packet, including its actual dose and error bounds. Qualify only two new
finite end strips per interior owner within the unchanged boundary band. Capture
one fresh complete ledger and require positive measured C gain/M reduction with
the original spill ceiling and caller precision. No actually laid cap is edited.

## Construction and ownership

Derive contour centres from the original four owned paths. A tighter requested
packet policy must not silently move that contour or discard its old proof.
Validate inherited gap/width bounds and count all retained/new roof segments and
packets against the original limits. Reuse the complete finite-footprint roof
solver on each new strip; the original body prefix remains its source. Combine
the three disjoint longitudinal pieces in order and recheck whole-cap amount
error. Original hatch lines/owners, target, ROI, later passes and boundary band
remain provenance; actual owned cap paths describe the new extents.

All capture, source walks, new roof/packet proofs, finite nominal band checks,
measurement and publication callbacks share the original root work/deadline.
The fresh ledger includes actual ordered packets and nonzero travel connectors.
No travel/contact/order certificate is inferred from capture. Publish neither
replacement nor replan snapshot on any refused later path or measurement.

## Disjoint union partition

The existing congruent loop kernel can still qualify the retained central
packets from the new ledger itself. Require complete congruent primary lists,
merged adjacent flat cores and the two constant transverse ends. Extra packets
must lie wholly outside the two end centre planes, on interior rows only.
Recompute the old loop measure from these actual central records; do not use
the previous cap's result as a new proof. Each transverse end has exactly half
its symmetric constant section outside the central interval. Therefore

    U_new = U_central_loop - (V_end0 + V_end1)/2 + U_left + U_right.

Left, central and right boxes have disjoint interiors and retain the original
XYZ limits. Each side union includes all actual intersecting records. Its
children share the original start time and remaining cells/work; refused child
work is also counted. Reserve at most 1/8 of the original requested width for
the central formula, then allocate the remaining measured width to both ends
with a one-percent arithmetic reserve. Recheck every final outward U/R width
against the unchanged caller limit. S is the complete exact ledger dose and
R=S-U measures total multiplicity, including triple occupancy.

## Bounded side integration

Retain exact individual mass when a box clips only the longitudinal extent of
an axis packet whose complete normal and Z sections fit, or bisects a constant
normal section at its symmetry plane. This proves clipped S only; actual finite
footprints and the original union/occupancy solver still determine U and R.

Local finite XY individual bounds also tighten multiplicity, even when global
S is exact. For complete positive profiles sharing a common vertical interval,
excess equals

    min_i(sum_{j != i} top_j) - max_i(sum_{j != i} bottom_j).

Concave tops/convex bottoms make this expression concave. Use transverse
Hermite-Hadamard only for qualified complete chains; a perpendicular participant
must be one constant-gap packet. A bivariate rectangle bound additionally
requires one complete packet per chain and commonly intersecting inner profiles.
An affine width/gap stadium loft is convex. Actual constant-flux width
w(h)=A/h+(1-pi/4)h is instead sandwiched by an affine tangent below and secant
above, using outward A/pi and positive h bounds. Convex inner/outer lofts bound
actual excess by corner-mean lower and midpoint upper quadrature. For exactly
one complete profile the same inequality bounds U directly. Never declare a
maximum of multiple roofs concave or the actual reciprocal-width loft convex.

Retain actual packet seams and holes. Exact packet cuts, group envelope faces,
gap/width uncertainty and comparison of actual alternative child bounds choose
refinement only. Discarded queries consume the same original limits. New
envelopes do not supply occupancy. Every final interval remains outward bounded;
unsupported geometry, depth/work/cell exhaustion, deadline or rounding refuses.

For three or more aligned parallel rows, a separate exact kernel requires
common affine top/gap, aligned interior-disjoint packets, merged adjacent flat
cores and ordered outer shoulders for the whole positive gap interval. With
outer centre span B, U per packet is (V_first+V_last)/2+B*L*(h0+h1)/2. Independent
113-bit flat sweeps and affine merged-core integration check unequal doses,
both axes and forward/reverse motion. This is not pairwise overlap summation.

## Consumers and boundaries

First-cap runtime contract becomes 2; new protected replan contract is 1.
Rebuild the application and both native test consumers. No persisted schema,
cache, profile, 3MF/IR format or public default changes. The run/event join
consumer selects the original contour crossing inside an extended infill path,
then proves its actual common lower volume with unchanged losses/limits. It
does not require the new endpoint to coincide with the contour centre. Original
per-event refusal and empty short lower packets remain tested.

The native complete policy remains 65535 cells, 2000000 work and 5 seconds, with
.001 mm3 output precision. Defaults remain unchanged. Smaller declared policies
can still refuse. Five new cases cover both axes, flat/sloped caps, preserved
central packets, independent amounts/union/multiplicity, wrapper capture, late
publication, budgets, rounding and a ridge outside the original footprint.
The native wedge rechecks all six run joins, every new flat-floor interface
packet and original target void partition. Original negatives are retained.

This reduces measured vertically clear deficit but leaves under-material voids
and repeated material. Permissible excess, complete shoulder/seam/3D fill,
actual later support, head/contact/order/flow, final-byte replay and full job
integration remain pending. Linux for this revision, Windows, independent safety
review and physical qualification remain NOT_RUN. Public export stays BLOCK;
full B01-B15 remains active. Commands and hashes are in B07-first-cap-end-replan.md.
