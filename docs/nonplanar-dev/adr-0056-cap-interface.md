# ADR-0056: Prove body anchors below every complete first-cap flat floor

Status: locally verified; independent review pending.

## Invariant

The prospective first cap must use its original immutable actual body prefix.
Require a positive-volume body D_lower box below the original support plane
over the entire source ROI. Every actual packet's complete varying flat floor,
expanded by original body/cap numerical uncertainty, stays above this anchor
within the declared support distance. Its separation from the actual highest
nominal body roof stays inside explicit gap/overlap bounds continuously.
The cap and future body cannot supply their own anchor.

## Decision

Add protected first-cap-interface contract 1 with a required explicit policy:
anchor depth, maximum support-plane separation, maximum nominal gap/overlap and
minimum flat-floor width. There are no calibrated defaults. These are declared
geometric criteria, not physical bonding, extrusion/contact qualification or
permission to print. Original upper/lower meanings and losses are retained.

Bind the cap's target proof to the same original body prefix and first-pass
proof. Reuse continuous D_lower coverage for the full 3D anchor; a plane-only
certificate cannot substitute for its required positive depth/volume. Retain
all active completed events and only the actual current fraction; future events
remain absent. Both original body and candidate record counts are bounded.

Derive flat-floor widths from each original binary amount divided by original
XY length and the actual affine gap. Rounded sections have half-width
(A/h-pi*h/4)/2; rectangular sections have A/(2h). Minimum width at maximum h
must meet the policy. The maximum width at minimum h supplies an outward outer
rectangle containing the complete variable floor. An inner strip alone cannot
certify the full floor. For refinement, enclose the complete local transverse
floor at the node's minimum h. Do not average flux, use nominal_width as actual
geometry or ignore packet boundaries.

Query the original highest nominal body roof over that complete local enclosure
expanded in XY by the summed original body/cap numerical errors. Charge the
same uncertainty on Z floor/roof separation and support distance. Expanded
floor bounds must remain inside the original anchored ROI. Reuse the continuous
roof bounds and exact rational longitudinal subdivision. All accepted leaves
together cover the complete packet; return one conservative packet-wide signed
separation interval. Coincident axes or point samples cannot supply a result.

Source capture/walk, D_lower coverage and every nominal roof visit/subdivision
share root cells/work/deadline and publication callbacks. The original current
endpoint and nominal outer AABBs only prune irrelevant rows. They never certify
support or contact. This reduces native work without weakening a predicate.
Return all packet results or none; preserve diagnostics on refusal. Mutable
caller wrappers/policy/limits cannot alter captured input.

Existing first-cap, material, run, integral and persisted contracts retain their
meaning. New protected snapshots own their original source and policy. No cache,
serialization, IR, 3MF/profile migration or public default changes. Rebuild all
application/native consumers. No body/target/native cap geometry, actual amount,
original material loss, contact exception or normative bundle changes.

## Evidence and boundary

Independent 113-bit whole-box lower and affine-floor/section bounds check actual
binary amounts and complete floor enclosures. Both cap axes and flat/sloped
surfaces pass. A partial future high roof outside the actual prefix remains
absent; completing it changes the expected floor. Coarse analytical segmentation
with numerical error 0.00348669 mm refuses the 0.003 mm interface allowance.
Positive sloped cases tighten width approximation from 0.005 to 0.002 mm;
neither the interface allowance nor other budgets are enlarged.

The unchanged native five-path/161-packet wedge proves a 0.03 mm3 body anchor
and all 161 varying floor interfaces. Original local cap joins, low-corner
per-event refusal, nominal fill and real residual deficit/spill remain. Exact
counts, failures and source/binary/raw/archive hashes are in B06-cap-interface.md.

This does not close the volumetric seam: rounded shoulders, complete union
connectivity, allowable multiplicity excess, actual remaining 3D voids and later
curved/stepped support still need construction/qualification. Physical bonding,
head contact/access, order/flow, final-byte replay and export remain separate.
Independent review, Windows and operator qualification are pending. Full B01-B15
remains active and public export remains BLOCK.
